Lingola – Privacy Policy  
Last Updated: 2025  
Welcome to Lingola. At Lingola, we take user privacy seriously; we are committed to processing your personal data in a secure, transparent, and controlled manner.  
This Privacy Policy is prepared to explain how the information collected through the Lingola mobile application and website (“Platform”) is processed, stored, and protected.  
By using Lingola, you are deemed to accept the practices outlined in this policy.  

1. Collected Information  
Lingola collects limited and necessary data to provide services and enhance user experience. The collected information is categorized as follows:  
a) Information Provided by the User  
   • Name, username, or profile information  
   • Email address  
   • App preferences  
   • Language selections and usage settings  
b) Automatically Collected Information  
   • Device information (device model, operating system, app version, etc.)  
   • IP address and basic interaction logs  
   • In-app usage and navigation information  
This information is used for the operation, development, and performance improvement of the application.  

2. Purposes of Data Use  
The collected information is processed for the following purposes:  
   • Account creation and management  
   • User authentication and secure login  
   • Personalization of the language learning experience  
   • Enhancement of app performance and stability  
   • Development of new features  
   • Prevention of security issues and abuse  
   • Provision of informational and promotional content with user consent  
Lingola processes personal data only for explicit, legitimate, and limited purposes. Personal information is not sold to third parties without your consent.  

3. Third-Party Services  
Lingola may work with third-party service providers that offer analytics, performance measurement, and technical infrastructure services.  
These service providers process data only on behalf of Lingola and within the framework of their own privacy policies.  
Third-party access is limited to improving user experience and ensuring the proper functioning of the application.  

4. Cookies and Similar Technologies  
Lingola may use cookies and similar technologies to remember user preferences, analyze performance, and enhance service quality.  
Details regarding the use of these technologies are explained in the Cookie Policy.  

5. Data Retention Period  
Personal data is retained only for as long as necessary for the purposes of processing.  
When legal obligations expire or the user account is deleted, data is securely deleted or anonymized within a reasonable time frame.  

6. Data Security  
Lingola implements technical and administrative security measures in accordance with industry standards to protect user data.  
Regular security checks are conducted against unauthorized access, data loss, or abuse risks.  
However, no data transmission over the internet can be guaranteed to be 100% secure. Therefore, it is important for users to be aware of this.  

7. User Rights  
Users have the following rights:  
   • Access to their personal data  
   • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data  
   • Request deletion of their account and data  
   • Withdraw consent for data processing  
These requests are evaluated through the support team.  

8. International Data Transfers  
Lingola may work with infrastructure providers that offer services on a global scale.  
This may require the transfer of data to servers located in different countries. In such cases, appropriate technical and legal protection measures are implemented.  

9. Policy Changes  
Lingola may update the Privacy Policy in line with technological developments or legal requirements.  
The current version will always be accessible through the app and website.
