Lingola – Cookie Policy  
Last Updated: 2025  
Welcome to Lingola. At Lingola, we use cookies and similar technologies to make your experience safer, more consistent, and functional when using the platform.  
This Cookie Policy is prepared to explain what cookies are, for what purposes they are used, and how you can manage your preferences.  
By using Lingola, you accept the use of cookies as stated in this policy.  

1. What are Cookies?  
Cookies are small data files stored on your device.  
They ensure the proper functioning of Lingola, remember your preferences, and help us understand how the application is used at a technical level.  
Cookies:  
   • Do not directly identify you  
   • Do not contain personal content  
   • Contain technical and usage-focused information  

2. What Cookies Do We Use?  
Lingola may use the following types of cookies:  
a) Essential Cookies  
These are necessary for the basic functions of the application to work.  
They ensure session management, security controls, and the proper functioning of essential system components.  
Without these cookies, some features of Lingola may not work properly.  
b) Performance and Analytical Cookies  
These help us understand how the application is used.  
They allow us to analyze which features are used more frequently and which areas need improvement.  
This data is evaluated in an aggregated and anonymous manner.  
c) Personalization Cookies  
These remember information such as language selection, appearance settings, or usage preferences to provide a more consistent experience.  
d) Marketing and Promotional Cookies  
With the user's consent, these support the more appropriate presentation of in-app content.  
In some cases, they may work in integration with third-party services.  

3. Why Do We Use Cookies?  
Lingola uses cookies for the following purposes:  
   • To ensure the secure and stable operation of the application  
   • To remember user preferences  
   • To conduct performance and usage analyses  
   • To improve service quality  
   • To make the user interface more consistent  

4. Third-Party Cookies  
Lingola may work with third-party providers that offer analytics or measurement services.  
These providers may use their own cookies, and these cookies are subject to the privacy and cookie policies of the respective service provider.  
Lingola uses third-party cookies solely for the purpose of enhancing service quality.  

5. Cookie Management  
Control over cookie preferences is yours. If you wish, you can:  
   • Limit, delete, or completely disable cookies from  
   • Device settings  
   • Application permissions  
   • Browser settings  
Note: Disabling essential cookies may cause some core features of Lingola to not function.  

6. Use of Data  
If the information collected through cookies qualifies as personal data, it will be processed under the Lingola Privacy Policy.  
Data will only be used within legal limits, for explicit purposes, and through secure methods.  

7. Policy Updates  
This Cookie Policy may be updated in accordance with technological developments or legal regulations.  
The current version will always be accessible through the application and website.
